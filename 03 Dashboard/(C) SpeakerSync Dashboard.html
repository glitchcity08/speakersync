Locked on Bluetooth

Keeping two Bluetooth speakers in time

SpeakerSync plays the Mac's audio on the Eris 4.5BT and the Brane, holds the faster one back, and listens through the MacBook mic to correct drift. This report covers one evening of measurements, taken from its own log.

Reported delay vs measured

milliseconds

The Eris tells macOS its Bluetooth delay is 94 ms. The mic shows it lags about as much as the Brane, which is why the first fixed delay of 273 ms made things worse.

Beep calibration runs

7 runs

A 1.5 kHz beep on the Eris and a 3 kHz beep on the Brane, half a second apart, timed by the mic. The Eris lands within ±5 ms every run; the Brane spreads out.

Drift corrections

Each dot is a correction. Above zero the Brane was late, below it was early. Diamonds are lock-ins at startup, where the Brane's delay resets. After the median-of-5 filter went in, short jumps stopped triggering corrections.

Bluetooth buffer swings

3 s windows

The Brane takes audio in bursts. Each bar is how far the Mac-side buffer swung in three seconds: about ±45 ms, while the speaker itself plays at a steady rate. Steering on this number made the early versions jumpy.

Version 1 resyncs

fixed delay

Version 1 held the Eris back by a fixed amount and kept the Brane's buffer level steady. Those bursts made it snap the Brane back 288 times.

The calculation

every ~0.75 s

What SpeakerSync computes from its three inputs: the system audio, the mic and each speaker's buffer.

// inputs s[n] system audio from the tap, 44,100 Hz m[n] MacBook mic, 48 kHz mapped onto s's timeline // what each speaker plays Eris s[n − erisD] fixed hold-back Brane s[n − lag] + shifts free-running // GCC-PHAT over a 1.5 s window C(f) = conj(M)·S / |conj(M)·S|^0.75 c(L) = |IFFT(C)|, L in 0–1.1 s, 150 Hz–7 kHz averaged across readings to beat room noise // find the speakers Pₑ = peak within ±12 ms of the last Eris P_b = peak within ±300 ms, more than 2 ms away echo if 3–12 ms behind a peak twice as strong // decide err = P_b − Pₑ > 0: Brane late med = median of the last 5 clear readings |med| > 6 ms slide the Brane 0.6 · med |med| > 50 ms jump straight there drift += 0.3 · err / Δt capped at ±1.2 ms/s
