Calibration

–
Sweep times each speaker alone with a 0.4 s chirp (music muted ~10 s). Loudness plays ~2 s of music on each alone so the app knows whose spike is whose. Buffers are rebuilt so every speaker lands with the reference. Live reads the mic every 0.25 s; a slipping follower is named by an inaudible 10 ms nudge and moved back. The sweep runs when SpeakerSync starts (every Bluetooth stream restarts then), when the speakers change, or when you press Re-calibrate. While you listen, a dropped stream, a macOS delay change or a spike no follower owns starts a quiet re-lock instead: each speaker, the reference too, is slid 10 ms in turn to find it in the music. Nothing is muted.
